Reflection Questions

1. What is your portfolio's total dollar gain or loss? What is your percentage return before fees?
2. After subtracting expense ratio costs, what is your net return? How much did fees cost you in dollar terms?
3. How did your individual stock perform compared to your index fund? What does this tell you about diversification?
4. How does your portfolio's return compare to the S&P 500 for the same period? (Search "S&P 500 performance [dates]" on Yahoo Finance.)
5. If you had put the full $10,000 into your worst-performing investment only, what would have happened? What does this demonstrate about concentration risk?
6. Would you change your portfolio if you could start over? What would you do differently and why?
